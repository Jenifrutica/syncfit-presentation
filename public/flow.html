SyncFit Edge — Adaptive routine flow

Two AIs: the trained local model synthesizes the hardware and DeepSeek designs the routine. The deterministic layer only validates and repairs, so every rule always holds.

3 · Routine designer

Reasons over the assessment, the profile, the gym machines and the evidence guidelines. Chooses patterns, exercises, order, sets, reps, rest and rationale.

  • Coverage of required patterns
  • No repeated movement pattern
  • Compounds before isolation
  • Machine-first + evidence dose
↓
validator

4 · Deterministic enforcement

Repairs and guarantees invariants before anything is shown or stored.

  • Normalize ids and map to catalog
  • Contraindications excluded (knee → no lunge; low back → no hinge)
  • Deduplicate patterns + required coverage
  • Filters: gym machines → free equipment (dumbbell/barbell/smith/bench) → bodyweight
  • Machine-first (photo + weight factor)
  • k_load echoed, never recomputed
↓

5 · Backend

Persists session, telemetry and routine; exposes REST/WebSockets. engine_used, machine_preferences and rationale in the response.

6 · Frontend

Adaptive routine with machine photo, exercise detail modal (metrics, estimated weight, pattern, why) and a Change button with the ordered alternatives list. Super admin has a users console; gym admin manages gyms and equipment.
