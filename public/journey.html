SyncFit Edge — user journey

From administration to a hardware-driven, AI-designed routine: seven stations of the real product flow.

01
Super admin

Creates the gym admin

Purely administrative: manages users/profiles (activate, deactivate, reset password, change role, delete). Does not create gyms or machines.

POST /admin/gym-admins/admin/users CRUD
02
Gym admin

Creates the gym & surveys equipment

Registers machines and free equipment (dumbbell, barbell, Smith, bench, cable) with a photo, weight factor, covered exercises and equipment type. Shares the join code/QR.

POST /gymsPOST /gyms/{id}/machinesQR
03
Athlete

Registers & onboards

Signs up with her cedula and completes onboarding: modality (cycle/pregnancy), goal, body composition, symptoms (pain 1-10) and training targets.

POST /auth/registerPUT /profiles/mesymptoms
04
Athlete

Joins the gym by code

The gym's equipment becomes available (live). She can switch the active gym if she belongs to several.

POST /gyms/joinGET /gyms/joined
05
Hardware

Scans biomarkers

PPG (100 Hz), dermal temperature and isometric force are captured; the local model (DSP + RandomForest) derives RMSSD, fatigue, phase and the deterministic load multiplier k_load.

PPG / °T / forcek_load ∈ [0.70, 1.05]
06
DeepSeek

Designs and validates the routine

DeepSeek (OpenCode Go) reasons from the assessment, profile, goal and gym equipment to design the session. A deterministic layer then enforces patterns, safety, equipment order and echoes k_load.

patterns · no duplicatescompounds firstmachines → equipment → bodyweight
07
Athlete

Trains, tracks & shares

Runs the routine (timers, per-set weight), swaps exercises, logs supplements and shares a read-only profile with her trainer.

WorkoutRunnerGET /exercises/{id}/alternativessupplements · shares
